About Our Province

Get to know the heart of the Zamboanga Peninsula

Geographic Location

Located in the southern region of the Zamboanga Peninsula in Mindanao, Zamboanga del Sur is bounded to the north by Zamboanga del Norte, to the south by the Moro Gulf, to the southwest by Zamboanga city, and to the east by Lanao del Norte. Its capital is the vibrant hillside hub of Pagadian City.

Rich Background & History

Historically home to the Subanen indigenous people, the province later embraced Muslim settlements and subsequent migration waves from the Visayas. It officially became an independent province in 1952. Over decades, it has blossomed into an agricultural and socio-cultural powerhouse, showcasing how diverse populations can build a unified home.

Why You Must Visit

Zamboanga del Sur offers an untouched natural beauty layout rarely congested by mass crowds. From Pagadian's famous iconic 45-degree angled tricycles to massive hidden water systems, cascading peaks, and unmatched warm hospitality, it's an authentic off-the-beaten-path paradise.

Culture and Traditions

Our culture is a wonderful quilt stitched from Subanen heritage, Islamic traditions, and Christian festivities. This blend presents itself beautifully in daily craftsmanship, colorful hand-woven clothing, traditional ritual dances, and inclusive regional celebrations.
