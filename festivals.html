Festivals & Cultural Events

Experience the vibrant rhythms and colorful celebrations of our people

September

Megayon Festival (Araw ng Zamboanga del Sur)

Location: Pagadian City (Province-wide)

The official week-long multi-cultural festival coinciding with the province's founding anniversary. 'Megayon' is a Subanen word symbolizing unity and solidarity. The event brings together the province's tri-people—Subanens, Muslims, and Christians—featuring breathtaking street dancing competitions, traditional arts crafts exhibitions, and agricultural trade fairs.

January

Pasalamat Festival

Location: Pagadian City

Held in honor of the city's patron saint, Sto. Niño, the Pasalamat Festival is a jubilant Thanksgiving celebration. Marked by heavy, continuous drum beats and beautifully customized costume parades, locals and tourists line the streets to participate in ritual dances thanking God for rich bountiful harvests and sustained peace.

July

Subayan Keg Subanen

Location: Lapuyan

Held in Lapuyan, historically known as the "Little America" of Zamboanga del Sur, this festival is a deep dive into the Subanen tribe's rich heritage. It highlights authentic traditional rituals, indigenous music, native dances, and the proud preservation of their ancestral customs.

Varies

Buklog Thanksgiving

Location: Province-wide (Subanen Communities)

Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage, the Buklog is a profound thanksgiving ritual of the Subanen people. The highlight of the event is the construction of a towering, elevated wooden structure. Community members dance in unison on the flexible platform, making it bounce rhythmically to express gratitude to the spirits for bountiful harvests, healing, and communal harmony.
