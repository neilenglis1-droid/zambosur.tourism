Local Culinary Delights

Discover unique culinary cross-cultures you must taste during your visit

Curacha with Alavar Sauce

A deep-sea spanner crab native to regional waters, steamed perfectly and covered with rich, sweet-spicy Alavar coconut milk sauce infusion.

Where to try: Local seafood markets and specialized restaurants in Pagadian City.

Satti

Grilled meat skewers served over sweet dense rice cakes submerged completely in a signature thick, boiling hot spicy red pepper sauce soup.

Where to try: Breakfast diners around center marketplaces in local urban hubs.

Lokot-Lokot

A crispy traditional dessert roll made from finely strained rice flour batter fried golden brown. Features an addictively crunchy textured design.

Where to try: Regional souvenir stands and Megayon cultural booths.

Chicken Pyanggang

An exotic, highly flavorful chicken dish cooked using blackened coconut meat paste mixed with rich native spices and lemongrass aroma compounds.

Where to try: Authentic Muslim heritage restaurants across municipalities.
